CAUSAL INFERENCE PROJECT BRIEF

1. The question and decision
What decision should this work change, who owns it, and what would count as a useful answer?

2. The intervention and its timing
What changed, when it started and ended, and which units were exposed? Note rollout, geography, or eligibility rules.

3. Available data and units
Which outcomes, units, and time coverage are available? Summarize frequency, known gaps, and who can share the data. Do not include personal records in an initial inquiry.

4. Existing experiments
Note any randomized tests, geo or lift tests, holdouts, or earlier analyses that could validate an estimate.

5. Timeline
When is the decision needed? Who will use the result, and are there procurement or review dates?

6. Open questions
What still needs to be resolved before scope can be agreed?
